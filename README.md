# CS-320
How can I ensure that my code, program, or software is functional and secure?

By accurately curating and writing accurate code that is efficient and fulfills the purposes asked; as well as with rigorous testing and reflecting, such as utilizing static and dynamic testing, breaking it apart, and making sure it intertwines and operates as needed. 

How do I interpret user needs and incorporate them into a program?

When beginning to work on programs, it is good to ask and accurately dig into what the customer wants or needs, and trying to imagine what branches, pathways, or other methods or inputs could happen, which can be difficult, but it is very needed when developing. We are trying to maintain goals, from every avenue of thought or input. 

How do I approach designing software?

I approach it with a few maintained ideas: to operate/function fully, without fail. This is the main goal, but also focusing on making this save and accurate even when pushed to it's limits is another goal to keep in mind. I try to break apart the main idea into bitesize pieces, with these ideas and morals as I am developing.
